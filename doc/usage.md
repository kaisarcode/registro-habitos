# Using Registro de Hábitos

Registro de Hábitos is intended for voluntary, quick activity logging. No mood, energy, duration, or other optional detail is required to save an entry.

## Record an activity

On **Registro**, choose a category or use the floating **+** action to open a new entry. Write a description or dictate it if speech recognition is available. Review the optional details and select **Guardar** to save.

Choosing a category prepares an entry but does not save it until you confirm. After saving, the feedback notification offers **Deshacer** for the latest entry.

## Edit an entry

Open an entry in **Historial** to edit its description, category, time, optional mood, energy, or notes. You can delete an entry from its edit dialog after confirmation.

For timed activities, **Inicio** is the start date and time. Enter **Horas** and **Minutos** to determine the end time, or choose **Fin** to calculate the duration. Intervals can cross midnight. Leave duration and end time empty when they do not apply.

## Find previous entries

**Historial** groups recent records under **Hoy**, **Ayer**, and weekdays, and older records under month and year headings. Expand **Buscar y filtrar** to combine text search with all dates, a selected year, a month, or an inclusive custom date range. **Limpiar** resets the filters.

The view loads matching entries in groups of 25 while you scroll. **Mostrar más** is also available.

## Manage categories

**Categorías** is a dedicated management tab. Search existing categories, add new ones with **+**, and edit names, keywords, colors, icons, or custom images. Removing a category does not delete previously saved entries.

## View summaries

The **Registro** screen includes a compact summary beneath **Hoy**. Choose **Ver resumen** for the full weekly, monthly, or yearly chart and category breakdown.

## Set reminders

Use the **Recordatorios** tab and **+** to create a reminder. Configure its time, schedule, and optional sound. Android may ask for notification or alarm access. Alarm timing depends on Android permissions and power management; force-stopping the app can prevent delivery until the app is opened again.

## Change preferences

Use the gear in the shared header to open **Configuración**. **Idioma** offers the device language, Spanish, or English. **Preferencias sensoriales** provides light/dark appearance and a **Sin animaciones** option. The Android reduced-motion setting is also respected.

Use **Respaldo y restauración** to preserve data outside the app and **Exportar para análisis** to share a records-only dataset. See [Data and privacy](data.md).
